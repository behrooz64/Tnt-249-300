Front and Rear Brakes / Rear Brake 
Rear Brake Caliper  
Disassembly: 
Note:

Note:

fastening bolt at the supporting plate of the brake 
caliper.

Brake cylinder piston (B), Fig. B

gasket)

extrude the piston at the right of the brake caliper,  
Fig. C
